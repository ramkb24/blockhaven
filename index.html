How to play

You can open this again any time with the Help button or the ? key.

Stick
WalkUse the round stick at the bottom left. In a car it drives.
Drag
Look aroundSlide one finger anywhere on the world.
Tap
BuildTap a block to build on it, or to use a door, oven, chest or car.
Hold
DigKeep your finger on a block for a moment to dig it. Or switch to Dig at the bottom.
Jump
Jump, swim and go upAlso climbs ladders, flies up and takes a lift up. Down does the opposite.
Menu
Buttons at the top rightMap, camera, saving, your look, cooking, the car workshop and the menu.

With a keyboard

↑↓
WalkHold Shift to run. You hop up steps by yourself.
←→
TurnHold the key longer to turn faster.
Space
Jump and swimPress Space to jump or swim up.
Drag
Look aroundDrag on the screen or swipe with two fingers.
Click
Build or digClick a block. Press X to switch between building and digging.
B
Build menuBlocks, over 100 items like beds, fridges and cars, and ready-made builds.
M
MapSee the whole island. Click a spot to travel there.
HV
Go home or to the villageH for the Buddy House, V for Maple Hollow.
C
CameraSee yourself from behind, from far away, or through your own eyes.
F
FlySpace to go up, Shift to go down. Press F again to land.
Z
UndoTakes back your last block or build.
E
Drive a carPut a car down from the Items tab. Click it or press E to get in. Arrows drive, Space brakes.
Click
Use thingsClick doors, gates, chests, gifts, the TV, the piano and cake to use them.
↑
Ladders and liftsWalk into a ladder and hold ↑ to climb. On a lift, Space goes up a floor and Shift goes down.
Q
Build bigQ builds or digs 3 × 3 or 5 tall at a time. Great for skyscrapers!
K
CookPick crops, raid the fridge, then cook at an oven, pot or campfire. Food gives you hearts and super powers.
L
My lookChange your hair, clothes, hats and glasses.
✦
Build a golemIron golem: 4 iron blocks in a T, then a carved pumpkin on top. Snow golem: 2 snow blocks, then a carved pumpkin. Click a golem to make it stay or follow.
G
Car workshopDesign your own car: type, colours, wheels, spoiler and a name.
⌘S
Save your gamePress Save at the top right (or ⌘ S) to save. Give it a name the first time. The game also saves itself.
✦
Crystal hunt12 glowing crystals are hidden on the island. Look for light beams in the sky!

Blockhaven

Who's playing?

Explore the island with Ram, Pip the fox and Bolt the robot. Visit the village of Maple Hollow, meet the villagers and their stone golems, hunt for 12 hidden crystals, cook, design your own car and build anything you like, even skyscrapers. At night the Gloomies hop out, so build a safe place or poof them with your friends.
